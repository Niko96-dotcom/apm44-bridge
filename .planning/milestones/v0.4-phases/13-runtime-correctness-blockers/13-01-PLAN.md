    Replace `MetricsPublisherState::snapshot` with atomic scalar storage for every field in `MetricsSnapshot`: `fillMs`, `smoothedRatio`, `ppm`, `underruns`, `overruns`, and `xruns`. Keep a sequence counter so `ReadMetrics` can retry until it observes a stable even sequence.

    Required shape:
    - `MetricsPublisherState` owns `std::atomic<uint64_t> sequence{0}`.
    - Each numeric field is stored atomically (`std::atomic<double>` or `std::atomic<uint64_t>` as appropriate).
    - `PublishMetrics` increments sequence to odd, stores all fields, then publishes an even sequence with release ordering.
    - `ReadMetrics` loads sequence, skips odd values, reads atomic fields, loads sequence again, and retries if sequence changed.
    - Do not introduce mutexes, allocations, logging, or blocking work in the publication path.

    Update tests so `MetricsPublisherSeqlockNeverDeliversTornSnapshot` still validates the stable-read contract without depending on a non-atomic payload. Keep or strengthen `NoBareMetricsSnapshotCopyInSource` so future direct cross-thread snapshot copies fail the source-level guard.
