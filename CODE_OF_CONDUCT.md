# Code of Conduct

APM44 Bridge contributors are expected to keep discussion practical, respectful,
and focused on making the project better.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but accept corrections quickly.
- Keep criticism specific to code, documentation, workflows, or behavior.
- Respect privacy and do not ask contributors to share private credentials,
  logs, or hardware details beyond what is needed to debug.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of signing keys, passwords, or user data.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may restrict participation
when needed to keep the project healthy.
